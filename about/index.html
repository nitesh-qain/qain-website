What we do

QAInfinity is a pure-play quality engineering and QA talent partner — we don't build software for clients, we make sure theirs works before it ships. That means manual, regression, and exploratory testing; framework-based and agentic test automation (including Loopsy, the autonomous AI QA platform we build and run in-house); and dedicated QA engineers and SDETs staffed on flexible terms, including a Contract-to-Hire trial option. See the full breakdown on our Services page.

Built for time zones outside India, the US, and the Middle East

Our positioning is deliberate: we work with product and engineering teams in the UK, Europe, Australia/NZ, and wider APAC who need QA coverage inside their own working day, not queued up for the next one. Engineers work in overlapping blocks with those hours, agreed before an engagement starts — not improvised after the contract is signed.

  • Fast, pre-vetted starts. Most engagements begin within 5–10 business days of a signed SOW — candidates are vetted before you ever see a profile.
  • NDAs and IP assignment as standard. Mutual NDAs and IP assignment are in place before any code or test asset is shared.

How we build capability

Engineers coming onto an engagement go through structured, ongoing training tied to the client's actual product, release process, and reporting standards — not a generic onboarding deck. On our Contract-to-Hire engagement with a real-money gaming client, that meant continuous feedback loops with the client's leadership and targeted, real-time upskilling on product functionality, release discipline, and defect reporting for every engineer on the team — the same discipline behind the 11-for-11 placement-to-conversion result in that case study.

Loopsy — the standard we hold ourselves to

Loopsy is our own autonomous AI QA platform, built in-house to generate, execute, and self-heal test suites across web, mobile, and API. We use it in our own automation engagements — it's the clearest proof point of the engineering bar every QAInfinity engagement is held to, not a third-party tool we resell.
