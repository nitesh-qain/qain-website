Contract-to-Hire: Reducing Risk in Technical Staffing

Stop paying the high price of bad technical hires. QAINfinity's Contract-to-Hire model lets you assess a candidate's technical skill and cultural fit in a real-world setting — significantly reducing your risk before you ever commit to a permanent hire.

~30%of a bad hire's first-year salary — the estimated cost of getting it wrong, per industry analysis

Hiring is expensive, and getting it wrong is more expensive still. Industry analysis puts the cost of a single bad hire at roughly 30% of that employee's first-year salary — a steep price for a poor fit or insufficient skills. And it's not a rare misstep: a reported 74% of companies admit to making at least one wrong hire. By any measure, traditional recruitment carries real, quantifiable risk.

The High Cost of a Missed Fit

In specialized fields, the right hire is about far more than passing a technical screen. Cultural fit, communication style, and long-term commitment matter just as much to whether a team actually succeeds. A candidate who looks flawless on paper can still disrupt workflow or never quite integrate — and the bill arrives later as costly turnover, re-hiring, and slipped project timelines. This is exactly where a smarter approach to vetting pays for itself.

De-Risking Your Next Hire with QAINfinity

QAINfinity's Contract-to-Hire model is a practical, low-risk alternative to direct hiring — built for the mission-critical roles you can least afford to get wrong. We draw on years of domain strength to pre-vet candidates for genuine, deep technical expertise, then hand control back to you through a simple process:

  • Select — choose a pre-vetted candidate from the QAInfinity talent pool.
  • Trial — we place the candidate on our payroll for an agreed contract period, so there's no permanent commitment on day one.
  • Assess — you evaluate their performance, technical capability, and cultural fit on real work, in your live environment.

You transfer the engineer to your own payroll only once you are completely satisfied with their performance. During the trial, the risk of the "unknown hire" sits with us — not with you.

Commit Only When You're Certain

By using Contract-to-Hire, you sidestep the financial and operational damage of a mis-hire — the direct and hidden costs of sourcing, onboarding, and training a replacement — keeping your focus on product delivery instead of re-running recruitment.
