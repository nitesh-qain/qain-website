Standing up a release team from scratch

MPL needed to build a reliable release team fast, and struggled to find engineers who were consistently reliable, honest, and hardworking through traditional hiring channels.

70%of MPL's release team is now sourced from QAINfinity

The Challenge

MPL was setting up a fresh release team and hit a wall common to fast-scaling engineering orgs: finding engineers who are consistently reliable, honest, intelligent, and hardworking is hard to filter for through resumes and interviews alone. Generic hiring pipelines were producing inconsistent results.

The Approach

QAINfinity supplied pre-vetted QA and release engineers matched specifically to MPL's release process and working style, rather than generic candidates sourced after the request came in. Engineers were evaluated against the actual demands of the release cycle, not just a generic QA skill checklist.

The Result

Trust compounded engagement over engagement. QAINfinity-sourced engineers now make up 70% of MPL's release team — a level of reliance that reflects how deep the relationship has gone, one placement at a time.
