How Contract-to-Hire Built a High-Performance QA Team — 11 Placed, 11 Converted

A leading real-money gaming platform needed to scale its QA capability fast, without taking on the financial and operational risk of committing to permanent hires before it was sure of them.

11 / 11QA engineers placed and converted to full-time payroll — 100% conversion, zero backfill

The Context

Hiring for a high-velocity domain like real-money gaming is hard. Releases ship fast, and a quality issue in production can hit revenue immediately. In that environment, a wrong hire isn't just a cost — it's delay, instability, and disruption a rapidly scaling company can't absorb. This platform needed to scale its QA capability quickly, without taking on the financial and operational risk of committing to permanent hires before it was sure of them.

The Challenge: Speed vs. Certainty

The client needed a strong, reliable QA team, but was reluctant to commit to full-time hires until it was confident on three fronts:

  • Technical depth — the ability to handle complex, gaming-specific testing scenarios.
  • Ownership and work discipline — dependability in a high-stakes, fast-moving environment.
  • Cultural fit — seamless integration with an existing fast-paced engineering culture.

Traditional hiring felt slow and risky. Interviews reward how well someone presents, not how they actually perform on a real release. The client wanted a model built on demonstrable performance, not first impressions.

QAINfinity's Structured Contract-to-Hire Solution

QAINfinity stepped in with its structured Contract-to-Hire model to de-risk the client's staffing. The premise was simple: let the client assess real fit and performance in a live-project environment before any long-term commitment.

From QAINfinity's vetted talent pool, the client shortlisted and onboarded 11 QA professionals. To ensure success and alignment through the contract period, QAINfinity layered in two forms of active support rather than simply placing engineers and stepping back:

  • Continuous feedback and alignment. We ran regular, detailed feedback loops with the client's leadership — continuously surfacing performance gaps, technical expectations, and process alignment specific to real-money gaming QA flows, so nothing drifted.
  • Real-time upskilling and training. Acting on that feedback, we delivered ongoing, targeted training to every engineer — on product functionality, release-process discipline, technical practices, and efficient defect reporting — keeping the whole team improving and integrating in real time.

The Outcome: Performance-Driven Commitment

Focusing on performance over prediction produced a clean result: all 11 engineers exceeded expectations during the contract period and were converted to the client's full-time payroll.

Why It Matters

An incorrect hire can cost an organization up to 30% of that employee's first-year salary (a widely cited industry benchmark). QAINfinity's Contract-to-Hire model removes that gamble: companies commit only after seeing a candidate's capabilities in action — making hiring safer, faster, and more reliable, and turning a risky permanent bet into an evidence-based decision.
