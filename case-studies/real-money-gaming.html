Case Study

Building a QA team with zero hiring risk

A leading real-money gaming company needed to scale its QA function fast, without absorbing the risk of a bad permanent hire in a high-stakes, compliance-sensitive product.

11 / 11QA engineers placed and converted to the client's payroll — a 100% conversion rate

The Challenge

Real-money gaming products carry real compliance and reliability stakes — a QA gap isn't just a bug, it's a regulatory and financial risk. The client needed to scale QA headcount quickly, but standard permanent hiring was too slow, and too risky to get right on the first try. A bad hire in this environment is expensive to discover and expensive to undo.

The Approach

QAINfinity placed QA engineers under a Contract-to-Hire model: engineers worked on the client's floor, under the client's own processes and tooling, for a defined evaluation period. Rather than a one-time interview loop, performance was assessed continuously — with ongoing training and feedback built into the engagement from day one, so both sides had a real, working relationship to evaluate before any permanent offer was made.

The Result

11 QA engineers were placed. All 11 were retained and converted to the client's payroll — a 100% conversion rate, with zero severance or re-hiring cost along the way. The client got a fully staffed, proven QA team without ever having to gamble on a resume.

"Contract-to-Hire let us evaluate real, on-the-job performance before committing — not just a resume and an interview."
