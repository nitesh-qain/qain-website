Frequently Asked

What clients ask before signing on.

How fast can a QA engineer actually start?

Most staff-augmentation placements start within 5–10 business days of a signed SOW — candidates are pre-vetted before you ever see a profile, not sourced after you ask.

How is Contract-to-Hire different from a staffing agency?

You work with the engineer for 30–90 days on our payroll, not yours, before any permanent commitment. If it's not a fit, you walk away with no severance cost or agency fee sunk.

Do you sign NDAs and handle IP protection?

Yes — mutual NDAs and IP assignment are standard in every engagement, before any code or test asset is shared.

Can your team overlap with our working hours?

Our engineers work in overlapping blocks with US, UK, and APAC time zones — scheduling is agreed before the engagement starts, not improvised after.

Do you use your own tools, like Loopsy, on client engagements?

Where it fits the stack, yes. Clients get the option to pilot Loopsy's autonomous QA agents alongside traditional automation — no obligation to adopt it.

How do I know if we actually need this?

Take our free Release Readiness Assessment — 13 questions, 2 minutes, no email required. It scores your actual risk across coverage, automation, performance, and team capacity, so you're not guessing.
