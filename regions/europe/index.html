A full working day of overlap, not a scheduled window

Because the time-zone gap between India and the UK/EU is small, engineers work standard daytime hours that already line up with your team's working day end to end — defects triaged, retested, and signed off in the same working hours as your own team, not queued for the next one.

  • No shifted schedules needed. Standard working hours already overlap — nothing to negotiate around a large offset.
  • Fast, pre-vetted starts. Most engagements begin within 5–10 business days of a signed SOW.

GDPR-aligned data handling

Every QAInfinity engagement already includes mutual NDAs and IP assignment before any code or test asset is shared. For UK and EU clients, we can also execute a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) on request and handle any client data or test assets shared during an engagement in line with GDPR data-handling principles — data minimisation, purpose limitation, and secure handling of anything shared for testing.
