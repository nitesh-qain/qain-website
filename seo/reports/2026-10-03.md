Every QA-outsourcing pitch promises "24/7 coverage." The question that actually matters is narrower: how many *live* overlap hours will you get, and what's the plan for everything outside them?

We did the actual time-zone math for UK, Singapore/Malaysia/Philippines, Japan/Korea, and Australia against a fixed India Standard Time (UTC+5:30, no DST) — and split it into two real scheduling models:

→ Natural overlap (UK, most of APAC): a standard India working day already covers your full working day. Nothing to negotiate.

→ Shifted block (Australia, same as our US engagements): the offset's big enough that we deliberately move the working block earlier — agreed at kickoff, not improvised.

Before you sign with any QA vendor, ask for the exact overlap window in writing, and what happens to a bug found outside it. "We cover your hours" isn't an answer. A start and end time is.
