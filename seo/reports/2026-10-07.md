## Research findings

No dedicated Phase 3 research-only day today (this was a Phase 4 execution day), but the sources used to ground today's post are new to the site's content backlog and worth flagging for future reference:

- **Rate gap, not just a perception gap:** Eastern-Europe nearshore QA rates run roughly double India offshore QA rates in 2026 vendor-rate surveys — a real number to have on hand the next time a "why not just use a nearshore team" objection comes up on a call. ([Bestarion](https://bestarion.com/offshore-vs-nearshore-outsourcing/), [QASource](https://www.qasource.com/blog/offshore-qa-vs-nearshore-qa/))
- **EU–India GDPR adequacy is still unresolved:** no formal European Commission adequacy decision exists as of early 2026 — the Commission has said India would qualify once it can show "essentially equivalent" protection, but that determination hasn't landed. This is a fact worth re-checking periodically (an adequacy decision, if it ever lands, would be a meaningful positioning shift for every UK/EU-facing page on the site, not just this post). ([IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/commission-requires-changes-before-adequacy-declared/), [ORF](https://www.orfonline.org/expert-speak/the-adequacy-dilemma-india-s-dpdpa-and-the-gdpr))
- **"Plus-one" hybrid sourcing is a emerging 2025–2026 pattern:** some buyers are pairing an offshore core team with a smaller nearshore slice specifically to split overlap-heavy and transfer-sensitive work between the geography suited to each, rather than treating it as all-or-nothing. Not actioned as a new task this run (would need a product-shape decision, not a content task), but worth the owner's awareness if a prospect raises it on a call. ([Bluecoding](https://www.bluecoding.com/post/is-nearshore-outsourcing-still-worth-it-in-2026))
