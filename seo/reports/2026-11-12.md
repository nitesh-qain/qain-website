## Research findings
Web search to ground the gaming page and post in current (2026) iGaming/real-money-platform QA practice:
- **Concurrency**: a new event or expansion can push concurrent players up by as much as 40%, enough to overwhelm a server pool sized for an ordinary day; iGaming traffic around major live events is described as extreme compared to normal traffic. ([QAwerk](https://qawerk.com/blog/game-performance-testing-optimize-multiplayer-game-performance/), [Ubertesters](https://ubertesters.com/blog/testing-for-online-casino-slot-games-igaming-platforms/))
- **Load testing scope**: should cover backend capacity, database limits, and matchmaking/settlement behavior under sustained and peak load, not just client responsiveness. A widely-cited "10,000 concurrent users, <0.2s response time" figure is a single vendor's own case, not an independently audited benchmark — flagged as such wherever referenced. ([QAwerk](https://qawerk.com/blog/game-performance-testing-optimize-multiplayer-game-performance/))
- **Payments**: sandbox gateways are a known blind spot — they can behave correctly while real cards and live fraud-filter responses surface failures the sandbox never would; a confusing payment error can prompt a retry pattern that trips a fraud filter on its own. ([Ubertesters](https://ubertesters.com/blog/testing-for-online-casino-slot-games-igaming-platforms/), [Global App Testing](https://www.globalapptesting.com/blog/gambling-app-testing-how-to-qa-online-casino-games-payments-and-compliance))
- **Device/OS coverage**: build the matrix from real usage data (activity, OS-version distribution) rather than a generic list, cover both older stable OS versions and the newest release, and keep real devices in the mix since emulators miss hardware-specific bugs; real-money betting apps in particular span a very large device/OS/network combination space. ([Snoopgame](https://snoopgame.com/blog/how-to-define-the-right-device-coverage-for-game-compatibility-testing/), [Global App Testing](https://www.globalapptesting.com/blog/cross-device-qa-for-real-money-betting-apps-in-2026))
- **Responsible gaming**: a missing or incorrect responsible-gaming message is framed as a real licensing risk, not just a UX gap — a practical reason to test age-verification, deposit-limit, and self-exclusion flows with the same rigor as a payment flow, even though the legal compliance determination itself sits with licensed compliance review. ([Ubertesters](https://ubertesters.com/blog/testing-for-online-casino-slot-games-igaming-platforms/))
- Caveat: sources are predominantly vendor/practitioner blogs rather than regulator documentation (UKGC, MGA, etc.); no RNG-certification or regulatory-standard claims were pulled from them onto the site, per this task's guardrail — only the shape of what functional QA should cover.
