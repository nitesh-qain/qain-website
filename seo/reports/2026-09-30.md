## Research findings
Research for today's post (not a dedicated Phase 3 day, but grounding for the blog content — web search, current as of 2026-09-30):

- **Fully-loaded QA-hire cost:** US mid-level QA engineer base salary $95K–$160K/yr, fully-loaded (benefits, payroll taxes, recruiting, tooling, ramp time) $130K–$235K/yr depending on source. UK base ~£40K–£65K/yr, fully-loaded roughly $74K–$102K/yr (National Insurance + pension add ~28%).
- **Outsourcing cost:** traditional agency testing commonly $15K–$50K/month for a small team; 2026 blended hourly rate surveys put India-based QA engineering at $18–$45/hr, Eastern Europe $35–$85/hr, US onshore contractors $80–$180/hr. Industry analysis puts typical savings from outsourcing at roughly 60–70% vs. the fully-loaded in-house cost.
- **Outsourcing risk (the side most vendor content omits):** ~70% of companies report bringing some previously outsourced work back in-house over the past five years; only ~50-54% report outsourced quality meeting or exceeding their internal benchmark; 50–70% of failed outsourcing engagements fail within the first 60 days; ~63% of companies cite data security as their top outsourcing risk.
- **In-house hiring risk (already used elsewhere on the site, reconfirmed):** a bad hire is commonly estimated to cost ~30% of that employee's first-year salary (US DOL), and 74% of employers admit to having made at least one wrong hire.

Sources cited inline in the post: [Stealth Agents — Cost of Hiring a QA Engineer 2026](https://stealthagents.com/research/cost-of-hiring-a-qa-engineer-2026) · [bug0 — In-house QA vs. managed testing: 2026 cost comparison](https://bug0.com/blog/in-house-qa-vs-managed-testing-cost-2026) · [FrugalTesting — QA Outsourcing: The Complete Guide for Engineering Teams (2026)](https://www.frugaltesting.com/blog/qa-outsourcing-the-complete-guide-for-engineering-teams-2026).
