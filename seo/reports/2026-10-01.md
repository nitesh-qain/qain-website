## Research findings
Research for today's post (grounding only, not a dedicated Phase 3 research day — web search, current as of 2026-10-01):

- **India IT attrition (FY26):** large IT services firms report last-twelve-month attrition in a 12.8%–15.1% band (HCLTech 12.8%, TCS 13.8%, Infosys 14.4%, Wipro 15.1%) — down sharply from the ~23% peak in 2022, but still a real retention risk that a mis-hire compounds.
- **Cost of a bad hire in India:** direct cost commonly estimated at 30–50% of annual CTC, with total impact (lost productivity, team disruption, backfill recruiting) reaching roughly one to two times annual salary for a mid-level technical role once everything is counted.
- **Contract-to-hire adoption in India is actively growing:** contractor engagements across Indian GCCs rose 46% between 2025 and 2026 while permanent hiring declined; roughly 1 in 4 roles at these organizations is now contractual, with some analysts projecting 1 in 3 by FY27 — a useful signal that the model this post explains is already where the market is heading, not a niche pitch.

Sources cited inline in the post: [Wisemonk — Attrition Rate in India 2026](https://www.wisemonk.io/blogs/managing-attrition-in-india) · [GroYouth — The Real Cost of a Bad Hire in India (2026)](https://groyouth.com/cost-of-a-bad-hire-india/) · [Ceipal — Contract vs. Permanent Hiring at GCCs in India (2026)](https://www.ceipal.com/resources/gcc-contract-vs-permanent-hiring-india-2026).
