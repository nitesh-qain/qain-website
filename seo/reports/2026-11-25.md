**Also found and fixed while in the release-readiness quiz markup** (in scope — it's the site's only multi-question form, and "form labels"/accessibility covers it): its 13 question/answer-group pairs had no programmatic association between a question's text and its radio-button options — a screen reader would announce "Documented, radio button" / "Partly, radio button" with no indication of which question those options answer. Fixed with `role="radiogroup"` + `aria-labelledby` on each `.answers` div, pointing at the matching `.q-text` paragraph. Also added `role="progressbar"` with a live `aria-valuenow` to the assessment's progress bar (previously a plain unstyled-for-ARIA `<div>`).

Fixing it cleanly means adjusting the brand gradient's end color, which is a visible brand-color decision, not a safe same-day AUTO change — flagging for the owner or a future REVIEW-lane pass rather than touching it tonight.
