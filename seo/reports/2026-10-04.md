**Draft post:**

Most launch-day outages aren't a bug nobody caught. They're traffic nobody tested for.

A manual check tells you the app works for one person, once. It tells you nothing about what happens when a Product Hunt feature, a press mention, or your own launch email sends everyone to the same page in the same ten minutes.

Before your next launch, run all four of these — not just one:
→ Load testing — does it hold up at the traffic you actually expect?
→ Stress testing — where does it actually break, and how gracefully?
→ Spike testing — can it survive a sudden surge, not just gradual growth?
→ Soak testing — what fails after hours of sustained load, not minutes?

The payoff isn't abstract. Rakuten's own Core Web Vitals data showed a good page-load score alone drove a 61% lift in conversion rate. A load test that fails three weeks out is a bug ticket. The same failure on launch day is an incident channel and a much harder conversation.
